5 reports read as a one-day move; filed under parser by tenant.

  • parser — shared date parser. It reads numeric dates month first even for tenants whose locale writes the day first. Keep #4471 with this cause. For example, report R-41200 describes an April date landing in July. Parse according to the tenant’s locale and test with an unambiguous day greater than 12 as well as a date whose day and month are both at most 12.
  • hint — booking-v3 date input. On the form, booking-v3 advertises MM/DD/YYYY. People follow the hint, then the form reads the input day first for the tenant. Track this in a separate ticket. For example, R-41230 came through the booking-v3 form for a tenant with a day-first locale. Generate the hint from the same locale and format used by the form’s parser.

How to triage new reports

Compare the cause before merging. Identical subjects can describe different failures: an Alder Physio booking-v3 form submission is a hint case, while an app report from the same tenant is a parser case. Different subjects can describe the same failure, for example the app and email reports from Kettle Books. If a tenant is missing or the cause cannot be established, leave the report for review before merging. Record the tenant, channel and cause when filing so the next triager can reproduce the decision.
